# Website FAQ

Copied from the old site, March 2025, before the move to the new shop platform.

**When do you roast?**
Every Monday. Orders ship on Tuesday.

**How much is shipping?**
Shipping anywhere in Portugal is €3.90, and free on orders over €40. We ship to Spain for €7.50. We don't ship anywhere else yet.

**How fresh is the coffee?**
We ship within a day of roasting. Every bag has the roast date on it. Coffee is at its best between one and four weeks after roasting.

**Can I get it ground?**
Yes: espresso, filter or cafetière. Whole bean keeps longer.

**What if my bag arrives damaged?**
Send us a photo and we'll replace it.

**Can I return coffee?**
Unopened bags can be returned within 30 days for a full refund.

**Do you sell wholesale?**
Yes, to cafés and restaurants in Porto. Email Inês.

**Do you do subscriptions?**
Not yet. Soon.

**Can I visit?**
We're not open to the public, but we run a cupping on the first Saturday of the month. Book by email, places are limited to eight.

**Is your packaging recyclable?**
The bags are paper with a compostable lining. The valve isn't. Pull it out and put it in plastics.
