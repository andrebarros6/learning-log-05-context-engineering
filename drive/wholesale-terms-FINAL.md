# Torrefação Lume — wholesale terms (FINAL)

June 2026. Agreed with Rui. This is the one to send.

## Ordering

- Order by Thursday at noon for the following Monday's roast, by email or on the WhatsApp group.
- The minimum order is 5 kg, in any mix of coffees.
- Delivery is on Tuesday, the day after the roast.

## Prices

Per kg, ex VAT:

- Casa: €21.00
- Ribeira: €25.00
- Single origins: in the weekly update

We give at least a month's notice before Casa or Ribeira change price.

## Delivery

- Free in Porto on orders over €30.
- Otherwise €6.

## Invoices and payment

- Each delivery comes with an invoice.
- Payment within 30 days of the invoice date, by bank transfer.
- More than 30 days overdue: we pause deliveries until it's paid.

## Damaged or missing coffee

Tell us within 7 days, with a photo. We'll replace it on your next delivery at no cost.

## Returns

No returns on roasted coffee, except damaged bags as above.

## Grinders and machines

We don't lend or repair equipment. Ask us for the people we use.
