## Notes

**Café Ponte.** Our first café. João is particular about grind and calls if the Casa tastes different. He pays, but often late. Always polite about it. Has his own grinder, a Mazzer, serviced by Delta Técnica.

**Ponte Nova.** Not the same as Café Ponte, and not related. Sofia opened in November 2025. Small, busy, mostly takeaway. Deliveries go to the side door on Rua do Cais, not the front. Had a split bag in April.

**Miradouro.** Hélder likes to try every new single origin. Good feedback, writes it down.

**Casa Bonjardim.** Biggest Casa order after Estação. Marta pays the day the invoice arrives.

**Leitaria Aurora.** Pastries first, coffee second, but Paulo cares. Switched to half Ribeira in the summer for batch brew.

**Pão de Cima.** Bakery. Rita closes in August.

**Café Vinte.** Nuno wants delivery before 9:00 or not at all.

**Tasca do Rio.** Lunch place. Small orders, regular.

**Estação.** Tiago orders the most and haggles the most. The Ribeira price conversation happens every quarter.

**Grão Fino.** Ana runs a proper brew bar. Filter only.

**Mercado 23.** Stall in the market. Rui (not our Rui) needs invoices addressed to the market company, Mercado 23 Lda.

**Sal & Brasa.** Restaurant. Newest. Filipa is still deciding whether to keep us after the trial.
