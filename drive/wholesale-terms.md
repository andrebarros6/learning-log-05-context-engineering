# Torrefação Lume — wholesale terms

January 2026

Thank you for stocking our coffee. These are the terms for cafés and restaurants that buy from us wholesale. We've tried to keep them short.

## Ordering

- Order by Thursday at noon for the following Monday's roast. Send orders by email to Inês or on the WhatsApp group.
- The minimum order is 5 kg, in any mix of coffees.
- We deliver on Tuesday, the day after the roast.

## Prices

Per kg, ex VAT:

- Casa (espresso blend): €20.00
- Ribeira (filter): €24.00
- Single origins: priced each week in the weekly update

Prices can change with green coffee costs. We'll tell you a month before any change to Casa or Ribeira.

## Delivery

- Free in Porto on orders over €40.
- Under €40, or outside Porto, delivery is €6.

## Invoices and payment

- An invoice comes with each delivery.
- Payment is due within 30 days, by bank transfer. The IBAN is on every invoice.
- If an invoice is more than 30 days overdue, we may pause deliveries until it's settled.

## Damaged or missing coffee

If a bag arrives damaged, or something is missing from the delivery, tell us within 7 days. Send a photo if you can. We'll refund the value by bank transfer within a week.

## Returns

We can't take back roasted coffee unless it arrived damaged.

## Equipment

We don't lend or service equipment, but we're happy to recommend people who do.

Inês and Rui
