# Suppliers and equipment

Rui's file. Who we buy from, what we run, and when it was last looked at.

## Green coffee

| Supplier | What | Terms | Contact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Origem Verde (Lisbon) | Brazil, Colombia, Ethiopia lots | 30 days, delivered | Pedro, by WhatsApp |
| Nordic Approach (via Hamburg) | East Africa, occasional Central America | Prepaid, pallet minimum, shared with two other roasters | Online portal |
| Café Raiz (Porto) | Small lots, samples | Cash on collection | Dona Fernanda |

Brazil Mogiana is the base of Casa. We buy a full year's worth in October when the price is fixed for the year. Colombia Huila is the other half of Casa and the base of Ribeira. Ethiopia rotates between Guji and Sidama depending on what Origem Verde has.

We keep about eight weeks of green coffee in the store room. The dehumidifier stays on at 55%. Check the hygrometer on Mondays.

## Roaster

Giesen W6A, bought second-hand in 2023 from a roastery in Braga that closed.

- Burner serviced every 6 months. Last service: 2 September 2026, by Termotec. Next due March 2027.
- Chaff collector emptied every roast day. Cyclone cleaned monthly.
- The afterburner fan bearing was replaced in May 2026. It was making the noise again in October. Termotec say it's fine for now.
- Profiles are saved in Cropster. Casa is profile C-14 since the May change. Ribeira is R-9.
