# Torrefação Lume — wholesale terms

October 2026

## Ordering

- Order by Thursday at noon for the following Tuesday's roast, by email or on the WhatsApp group.
- The minimum order is 3 kg, in any mix of coffees.
- Delivery is on Wednesday, the day after the roast.

## Prices

Per kg, ex VAT, listed in each weekly update. We give at least four weeks' notice before Casa or Ribeira change price.

## Delivery

- Free in Porto on orders over €30.
- Otherwise €6.

## Invoices and payment

- Each delivery comes with an invoice.
- Payment is due 14 days after the invoice date, by bank transfer.
- We send one reminder the day after the due date.
- A late fee of 2% applies to any invoice still unpaid 30 days after its due date. We'd much rather not charge it, so tell us if something's holding a payment up.

## Damaged or missing coffee

Tell us within 48 hours of delivery, with a photo. We credit the full value against your next invoice. We don't make cash refunds on wholesale orders.

## Returns

No returns on roasted coffee, except damaged bags as above.

## Grinders and machines

We don't lend or repair equipment. Ask us for the people we use.

Inês and Rui
