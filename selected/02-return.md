# Shop policies

Last updated 2 September 2026. This is what's on the website under Help.

## Shipping

- We roast on Tuesdays and ship on Wednesdays, by CTT.
- Portugal (mainland, Madeira and the Azores): €4.50. Free on orders over €35.
- Rest of the EU: €9.00.
- We don't ship outside the EU.
- Orders placed after Tuesday at 10:00 ship the following Wednesday.

## Grinds

Whole bean, or ground for espresso (machines and moka pots), filter (V60, AeroPress, drip machines) or cafetière. Choose at checkout.

## Damaged bags

If a bag arrives damaged, email us a photo. We'll send a new one at no cost, or refund it if you'd rather. You don't need to send anything back.

## Returns

Unopened bags can be returned within 14 days for a refund. Postage back is yours to pay.

## Subscriptions

Every two or four weeks, any coffee, any grind. Pause or cancel from your account, up to the Monday before a roast.

## Gift cards

€25, €50 or €100. Valid for a year.

## Contact

Email Inês at shop@torrefacaolume.pt. We answer within two working days.
